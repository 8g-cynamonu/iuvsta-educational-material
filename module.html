Module 1

Fundamentals of Vacuum

A ready-to-deliver teaching package covering the physical principles underpinning vacuum science. Authored by John F. O'Hanlon for the IUVSTA Visual Aids Program, 2nd edition (2004). The package consists of a PowerPoint slide deck with detailed slide-by-slide presenter notes.

Section 1 — Gas Kinetics
  • Maxwell–Boltzmann velocity distribution and molecular speeds
  • Mean free path and particle flux
  • Monolayer formation time
  • Dalton's Law and partial pressures
Section 2 — Gas Laws
  • Boyle's, Gay-Lussac's and Charles' Laws
  • Avogadro's number and the ideal gas law
  • Adiabatic change of state
  • Van der Waals gas and condensation
Section 3 — Gas Transport
  • Diffusion: Fick's first and second laws
  • Viscosity: concept and pressure dependence
  • Thermal conductivity and heat conduction
  • Thermal transpiration
Section 4 — Gas Flow
  • Choked, turbulent, viscous and molecular flow regimes
  • Hagen–Poiseuille law and short-tube models (Santeler)
  • Conductance, transmission probability and pumping speed
  • Combining components: Oatley and Haefer methods
Section 5 — Gas Desorption from Surfaces
  • Physisorption and chemisorption
  • Langmuir, BET, DRK and Temkin adsorption isotherms
  • Permeation and activation energies
  • Zeolites and sorption materials
Intended audience: Lecturers and instructors delivering introductory vacuum engineering courses. Slide-by-slide presenter notes provide key talking points, equations and physical context for each slide. Recommended for use alongside a vacuum engineering textbook.
